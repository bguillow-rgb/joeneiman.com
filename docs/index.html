Joe Neiman

I'm an average guy who has been able to achieve some pretty incredible outcomes through a combination of thinking big, trusting my gut, learning from failures and mistakes, pushing the boundaries of what is reasonable or realistic, trying hard, and plenty of luck along the way.

I started buying and selling cars in my parents' driveway at 15 years old. That turned into a dealership, then into co-founding ACV Auctions, and later founding DealerClub. These days I fly, I look for good deals, I support causes I believe in, and I'm working on turning two decades of lessons into a book.

What I'm looking for

I'm building a portfolio of franchise car dealerships because I believe in the resiliency of the US auto industry and the cultural stronghold that automobiles hold. I buy and sell airplanes. I'm interested in early stage companies doing neat things with health and wellness, marketplaces, and transportation, and I am always interested in air-cooled Porsches and other specialty cars and trucks. If you've got one of those, I want to hear about it.
